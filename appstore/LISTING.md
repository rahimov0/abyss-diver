Dive into the deep in Abyss Diver, a retro pixel-art arcade adventure inspired by the classic mobile diving games.

Swim through 75 hand-crafted levels across 11 underwater worlds — from sunny coral shallows and kelp forests to frozen trenches, volcanic rifts, the neon ruins of Atlantis, a toxic trench and an abandoned submarine base. Every level has its own layout and its own surprise.

• Collect every gem, then race to the exit portal

Start playing immediately — no account needed. If you want to keep your progress across devices, you can optionally sign in with Apple or Google in Settings. No ads, no tracking.

## App Review notes

> Abyss Diver is fully playable without an account: tap CONTINUE / START on the title screen.
> Signing in is optional and only syncs game progress (Settings → SIGN IN WITH APPLE or SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE).
> Account deletion: Settings → DELETE ACCOUNT (tap twice to confirm). It deletes the Firebase account and the cloud save; Apple sign-in tokens are revoked.
> No demo account is needed. Contact: destek.rahimov@gmail.com
